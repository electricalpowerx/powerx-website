Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to rewire my whole house for smart lighting?
Not always. Many modern systems work with existing wiring or use wireless technology. For the best long-term results in new construction, we recommend proper pre-wiring during the build.
Which smart home platforms do you work with?
We have experience with Lutron, Control4, Crestron, and other professional systems, as well as more accessible options using Wi-Fi and Zigbee devices. We recommend the best fit for your needs and budget.
Can smart lighting save energy?
Yes. Smart scheduling, occupancy sensing, and daylight harvesting can significantly reduce lighting energy use while improving comfort and convenience.
