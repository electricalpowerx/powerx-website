Level 1 vs Level 2 Charging

For most EV owners, a Level 2 charger is the right choice.

Level 1 (120V)

Plugs into a standard outlet and adds roughly 5-8 km of range per hour. Fine as a backup, but too slow for most daily drivers.

Level 2 (240V)

Adds about 30-50 km of range per hour and fully charges most EVs overnight. This is the standard for home charging.

Smart Chargers

Wi-Fi enabled models let you schedule charging for off-peak rates, track energy use, and balance loads with the rest of your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does EV charger installation cost in Surrey?
Cost depends on the distance from your panel to the charger, whether a panel upgrade or load management device is needed, and the charger model. A simple install near the panel is very affordable; longer runs cost more. We give a free, fixed quote after a quick site assessment.
Do I need a panel upgrade to install a Level 2 charger?
Not always. Many homes can add a Level 2 charger to their existing panel, sometimes with a smart load-management device that avoids a full upgrade. If your panel is full or undersized we will tell you honestly and quote both options.
Which EV chargers do you install?
We install all major brands including Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, Wallbox, Grizzl-E, FLO and JuiceBox. If you have not bought one yet, we will recommend the best fit for your vehicle, panel and budget.
Are there rebates for EV chargers in BC?
Rebate programs through BC Hydro and other agencies change periodically. We stay current on what is available and help you choose equipment that qualifies, but we recommend confirming the latest rebate details directly with the program before purchase.
Do you handle the permit for EV charger installation?
Yes. We pull the electrical permit, install to the current BC Electrical Code, and arrange the Technical Safety BC inspection so your charging station is safe, compliant, and ready to use.
