Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work after hours to avoid disrupting my business?
Yes. We regularly schedule commercial work during evenings, weekends, and off-peak hours to keep your operations running. We will plan the work around your business so downtime is minimized or eliminated.
Can you handle tenant improvements and retail fit-outs?
Absolutely. Tenant improvements and retail fit-outs are a core part of our commercial work — new lighting, power distribution, data, signage circuits, and everything needed to get your space open on schedule and to code.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance contracts?
Yes. We offer scheduled preventive maintenance and priority service agreements for property managers, strata, retail, and industrial clients who want a reliable electrician on call and fewer surprise failures.
Are you familiar with commercial code requirements?
Our electricians work to the current BC Electrical Code and understand the additional requirements for commercial and industrial occupancies, including emergency lighting, exit signage, fire alarm interfacing, and inspection coordination.
What size commercial jobs do you take on?
From a single circuit or lighting retrofit to full building power upgrades and multi-unit fit-outs, we scale to the project. Contact us with your scope and we will advise on timeline and approach.
