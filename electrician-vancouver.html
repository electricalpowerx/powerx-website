Local Knowledge of Vancouver

Many Vancouver homes east of Main Street and through Kitsilano and Mount Pleasant were built before modern wiring standards, which means knob-and-tube and ungrounded outlets are common — and a frequent flag during home sales and insurance renewals. We handle these updates regularly and know what inspectors and insurers look for.

Downtown and the West End bring a different challenge: high-rise condos and strata buildings where coordination, clean work, and code compliance matter. From Yaletown to Commercial Drive’s small businesses, we tailor our approach to each property and keep disruption to a minimum.

Because Vancouver traffic and bridge crossings can affect arrival times, we’re upfront about response windows and keep you informed from the moment you call.
