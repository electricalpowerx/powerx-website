Frequently Asked Questions

How much does landscape lighting cost?
Pricing depends on the size of the property, number of fixtures, and whether the system is low-voltage LED or smart-enabled. Most residential projects range from a few thousand dollars upward. We provide detailed quotes after a site visit.
Do you install low-voltage or line-voltage systems?
We primarily install low-voltage LED systems for landscape lighting because they are safer, more energy efficient, and easier to control. We can also do line-voltage architectural lighting where required.
Can landscape lighting be added to an existing home?
Yes. While it is ideal to plan during new construction, we regularly install beautiful landscape lighting systems on existing properties with minimal disruption.
