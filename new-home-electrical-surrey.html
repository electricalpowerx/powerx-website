Frequently Asked Questions

When should electrical rough-in happen during construction?
Rough-in typically happens after framing and before insulation and drywall. This is the best time to run all wiring cleanly and make changes easily.
What panel size should I get for a new home?
Most new homes today should have at least a 200A service. If you plan for EV charging, heat pumps, or a generator, we often recommend planning for 200A or even 400A service from the start.
Can you help with lighting design?
Yes. We work with builders and interior designers to plan beautiful, functional lighting layouts including recessed lighting, decorative fixtures, and smart controls.
Do you work directly with builders?
Absolutely. We regularly partner with home builders across Surrey and the Lower Mainland for full electrical packages on new construction projects.
