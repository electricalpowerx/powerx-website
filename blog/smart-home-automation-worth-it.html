Is Professional Smart Home Lighting Worth It in 2026?

Smart lighting has gone from a novelty to a common request in new homes across Surrey. Here’s a realistic look at whether it’s worth the investment today.

The Benefits Are Real

  • Beautiful, consistent lighting scenes with one tap
  • Energy savings through scheduling and dimming
  • Improved security with automated “away” modes
  • Convenience — especially useful for larger homes
  • Increased resale appeal for tech-savvy buyers

The Reality Check

Cheap Wi-Fi bulbs and switches often create more problems than they solve (connectivity issues, poor reliability, limited features). Professional systems (Lutron, Control4, or well-implemented higher-end platforms) perform much better but cost more upfront.

Our Recommendation

If you’re building new or doing a major renovation, professional smart lighting control is usually worth it — especially if you value convenience and design. For existing homes, we often recommend starting with key areas (main living spaces + outdoor lighting) rather than trying to automate everything at once.

Bottom Line: Done properly, smart lighting improves daily life and adds real value. Done cheaply, it often leads to frustration.
