What to Do When the Power Goes Out at Night in Surrey

Published • Emergency Preparedness

A sudden power outage at 2 a.m. can be alarming — especially during a winter storm in Surrey. Here’s exactly what to do while you wait for help to arrive.

1. Stay Calm and Check Your Breakers First

Before assuming it’s a widespread outage, check your main electrical panel. A single tripped breaker is very common and easy to reset.

2. Use Safe Lighting Only

Avoid candles if possible. Use flashlights or battery-powered lanterns instead. Never leave candles unattended, especially with children or pets in the house.

3. Unplug Sensitive Electronics

Power surges when the electricity comes back on can damage computers, TVs, and gaming consoles. Unplug anything expensive until power is restored.

4. Keep Your Freezer and Fridge Closed

A full freezer can stay cold for 24–48 hours if left unopened. This helps prevent food spoilage during longer outages.
