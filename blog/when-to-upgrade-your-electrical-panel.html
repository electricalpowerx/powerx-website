5 Warning Signs Your Electrical Panel Needs an Upgrade

If your home was built before the 1990s, there’s a good chance your electrical panel is undersized for modern living. Here are the most common warning signs we see in Surrey homes.

  1. Frequent breaker trips — especially when running multiple appliances.
  2. Flickering or dimming lights when the furnace, dryer, or microwave turns on.
  3. Warm or hot breaker panels — a serious safety concern.
  4. Aluminum wiring or fuse boxes still in use.
  5. Not enough circuits for today’s devices (EV chargers, home offices, AC, etc.).

Most 1970s–1980s homes in Surrey still have 100A or even 60A services. Upgrading to a 200A panel is one of the best investments you can make for safety and future-proofing.
