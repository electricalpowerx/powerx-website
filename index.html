Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you really get to me in an emergency?
We target 30–60 minutes in most parts of Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby and nearby areas. Severe weather or peak periods may extend this slightly, but we always communicate clearly.
Are your electricians licensed and insured in British Columbia?
Yes. Every technician holds a valid BC electrical license and we carry full commercial liability insurance. We work strictly to the current BC Electrical Code.
Do you charge extra for nights, weekends, or holidays?
We provide clear upfront pricing before any work begins. While emergency calls have a dispatch fee, we do not inflate rates dramatically after hours the way some companies do.
What areas do you serve?
All of Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, Langley, White Rock, Maple Ridge, and most of the Lower Mainland. Call us — we’ll confirm coverage for your exact location.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. We provide free written estimates for all non-emergency work. For emergencies we give a clear price on site before starting.
What should I do right now if I smell burning or have no power?
Turn off the main breaker if it’s safe, then call us immediately at 778-823-1575. If you smell gas or see flames, call 911 first.
