Why Homeowners Upgrade Their Electrical Panel

Most homes built before the 1990s were wired with 100-amp service. Today’s homes need significantly more power for air conditioning, electric vehicle chargers, hot tubs, and modern appliances.

A panel upgrade is often the first major electrical investment homeowners make when adding high-load devices or renovating.

100A Panel (Old Standard)

  • • Limited capacity for modern loads
  • • Frequent breaker tripping
  • • Cannot support EV chargers or heat pumps
  • • Insurance companies increasingly require upgrades

200A Panel (Modern Standard)

  • • Supports EV chargers + heat pumps
  • • Room for future expansion
  • • Meets current electrical code
  • • Increases home value and insurability
