Residential Wiring & Rewiring in Surrey

Old or damaged wiring is one of the most common — and most overlooked — hazards in a home. PowerX Electrical provides complete residential wiring and rewiring services across Surrey and the Lower Mainland, from updating a few unsafe circuits to full whole-home rewires in older houses.

If your home still has knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring, two-prong outlets, or a panel that struggles to keep up, our licensed electricians can bring it up to modern, safe, code-compliant standards. We also handle wiring for renovations, additions, kitchens, bathrooms, and EV readiness.

When to Consider Rewiring

These are the situations where rewiring is most often needed:

✓Knob-and-tube wiring (common before the 1950s)
✓Aluminum branch wiring (1960s-1970s homes)
✓Two-prong, ungrounded outlets throughout
✓Renovations, additions, or a new kitchen/bathroom
✓Insurance requiring an electrical update
✓Frequent tripping, flickering, or warm outlets

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my house needs rewiring?
Warning signs include knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring, two-prong ungrounded outlets, frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, warm switch plates, a burning smell, or a home over 40 years old that has never been updated. An assessment confirms what is needed.
Is aluminum wiring dangerous?
Aluminum branch wiring from the 1960s-70s can be a fire risk at connections, where it loosens and overheats over time. It is also an insurance concern. We remediate or replace aluminum wiring to make your home safe and insurable.
Do I have to move out during a rewire?
Usually not. We stage the work room by room to keep your home livable, restoring power to finished areas each day. For larger whole-home rewires we will discuss the schedule and any short outages with you in advance.
How much does rewiring a house cost in Surrey?
Cost depends on the size of the home, accessibility, and how much wiring is being replaced. A partial rewire of a few circuits is modest; a full whole-home rewire is a larger project. We provide a detailed written quote after assessing your home.
Do you repair drywall after rewiring?
We work to minimize wall openings and make clean, patchable cuts. We can coordinate drywall patching or leave openings ready for your finisher — just let us know your preference when we quote the job.
