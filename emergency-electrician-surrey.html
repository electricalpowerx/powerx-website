Common Electrical Emergencies We Handle

If you are experiencing any of the following, stop using the affected circuit and call us right away. These are the issues we respond to most often:

✓Complete or partial power loss in your home or building
✓Burning smell, hot outlets, or scorch marks around switches
✓Sparking, buzzing, or arcing from outlets and panels
✓Breakers that trip repeatedly or will not reset
✓Electrical damage from storms, flooding, or fallen lines
✓Exposed, damaged, or smoking wiring

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can an emergency electrician reach me in Surrey?
In most parts of Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby and the surrounding Lower Mainland we aim to be on site within 30 to 60 minutes. We keep our vans fully stocked so we can diagnose and fix most issues on the first visit, day or night.
Do you charge extra for nights, weekends or holidays?
We give you a clear price before any work starts. Emergency calls include a dispatch fee, but we do not inflate our hourly rates dramatically after hours the way some companies do. You will always know the cost up front.
What should I do right now if I smell burning or have no power?
If it is safe, switch off your main breaker, unplug affected devices, and call us immediately at 778-823-1575. If you see flames, sparks that will not stop, or smell gas, call 911 first, then call us.
Are your emergency electricians licensed and insured?
Yes. Every PowerX technician holds a valid British Columbia electrical licence and we carry full commercial liability insurance. All emergency repairs are completed to the current BC Electrical Code.
What types of electrical emergencies do you handle?
Power outages affecting only your home, burning smells, sparking outlets, repeatedly tripping breakers, dead circuits, panel failures, storm and water damage, and any situation that feels unsafe. When in doubt, call us.
